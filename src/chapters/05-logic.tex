\chapter{Platform and Business Logic}\label{ch:logic}
\chapterinfo{On-device, backend (in scope only)}{\mtop{M3} \mtop{M4} \mtop{M6}; \masvs{AUTH} \masvs{PLATFORM}}

The defects that matter most to a client are often not in a library but in how the app's own workflows can be abused. These need the white-box understanding of the feature plus hands-on manipulation of the running app and its API.

\begin{objectives}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt]
\item Test authentication flows: lockout, brute force, and biometric fallback (\mtop{M3}).
\item Test transaction and payment logic for client-trust and replay (\mtop{M3}, \mtop{M4}).
\item Test multi-step workflows for step-skipping and state manipulation.
\item Check privacy leakage through notifications and third-party flows (\mtop{M6}).
\end{itemize}
\end{objectives}

\section{Authentication flows}
\begin{findingbox}
A login, PIN, or biometric gate that can be worn down or sidestepped. (\masvs{AUTH-1})
\end{findingbox}
Test account lockout and server-side rate limiting on login and on any PIN or OTP entry. Check that a local PIN or pattern is verified server-side where it protects server data, and that biometric authentication is bound to a Keystore key use rather than a boolean the app can be made to skip. Test the fallback path: an app that falls back from biometric to a weak secret is only as strong as the fallback.

\section{Transaction and payment logic}
\begin{findingbox}
The server accepts a value or outcome the client should not be trusted to set. (\masvs{AUTH-3})
\end{findingbox}
Where the app handles money or entitlements, test whether the client is trusted for price, amount, quantity, or currency, and whether a purchase or transfer can be replayed or re-ordered. Intercept and modify the relevant requests within scope and confirm whether the server independently validates the result.

\section{Workflow and state manipulation}
\begin{findingbox}
A required step that can be skipped, or application state the client can set past a check. (\masvs{AUTH-3})
\end{findingbox}
For multi-step flows (registration with verification, checkout, KYC), test whether a later step can be reached without completing an earlier one, whether a verification step can be omitted, and whether app state carried to the server can be manipulated to bypass a gate.

\section{Privacy and third-party surfaces (\mtop{M6})}
\begin{findingbox}
PII exposed through platform surfaces or over-shared with third parties. (privacy, \masvs{PLATFORM})
\end{findingbox}
Check notification content on the lock screen, data sent to analytics and advertising SDKs against what the privacy policy states, and the handling of tokens in third-party authentication (OAuth) redirects. Over-collection and silent sharing are reportable privacy findings even when no classic vulnerability is present.

\begin{summarybox}
\begin{itemize}
\item Prove server-side rate limiting and lockout; test the biometric fallback, not just the happy path.
\item For money and entitlements, confirm the server validates value and guards against replay.
\item Test whether required workflow steps can be skipped or state manipulated past a check.
\item Treat lock-screen leakage and silent third-party sharing as reportable privacy findings.
\end{itemize}
\end{summarybox}
